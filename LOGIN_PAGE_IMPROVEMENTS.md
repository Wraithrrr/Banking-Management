Enhanced the login page to better showcase the enterprise automation and workspace management capabilities of the software.

## Design Philosophy
The new design emphasizes:
1. **Active Work Environment**: Shows work being processed in real-time
2. **Automation Focus**: Highlights AI and workflow automation capabilities
3. **Enterprise Scale**: Statistics showing large-scale operations
4. **Professional Polish**: Smooth animations and modern UI patterns
5. **Trust & Security**: Maintains enterprise-grade security messaging

## Color Scheme
- **Primary**: Emerald/Green (automation, growth, success)
- **Secondary**: Sky Blue (technology, reliability)
- **Accents**: Purple, Orange, Green (different departments/activities)
- **Background**: Dark slate with subtle patterns
